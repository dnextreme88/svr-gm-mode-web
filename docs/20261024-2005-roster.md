# 2005 roster and "Use WWE roster"

## Context

The seeded catalog (`database/seeders/data/wrestlers.php`) was the original spec roster of 110, with placeholder brands that did nothing. This change:

1. adds everyone else signed to WWE in 2005, from a 2005 roster list on thesmackdownhotel.com (weights and classes from there);
2. sets the brands to the real WWE rosters of **Nov 14, 2005** (SvR 2006's release; [Raw](https://www.thesmackdownhotel.com/roster/?promotion=wwe&date=2005-11-14#raw), [SmackDown](https://www.thesmackdownhotel.com/roster/?promotion=wwe&date=2005-11-14#smackdown)), trimmed to 30 each;
3. adds a new-game choice between **Manual draft pick** (the initial draft as before) and **Use WWE roster** (each brand signs its real roster).

## Rules changed (2026-10-24)

- **New Game → Rosters:** _Manual draft pick_ (default) runs the initial draft as before; the pool is everyone who isn't a Legend, brand or not. _Use WWE roster_ skips the draft: each brand signs every wrestler the catalog places on it (30 each), and the save goes straight to title assignment (the rival crowns its champions as usual).
- WWE-roster signings work exactly like draft picks: the 52-week draft contract, its signing fee out of the $15M budget, Happy morale. Contract values are still the deferred placeholders.
- Everyone without a brand (free agents and all Legends) starts in Free Agency in both modes.
- `DraftMode` enum (`manual`, `wwe_roster`); `NewGameService::create(..., $mode)` calls `DraftService::signWweRosters`. `POST /gm/saves` takes an optional `draft_mode` and returns `next_url` (the draft room, or title assignment).
- `/gm/docs` has a new **Rosters & draft** tab: both modes side by side (cap, budget, contract length, Legends and the rival GM's pick rule from config) and the two WWE rosters, colour-coded.
