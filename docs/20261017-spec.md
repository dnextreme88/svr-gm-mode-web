# SmackDown vs Raw 2006 — GM Mode Mechanics Spec

The first iteration of GM Mode. Everything below is scoped to what shipped in 2006 — no momentum bar, no weekly grading UI, no angle system. Those came in later THQ entries.

**House rules (deliberate deviations from the 2006 game):**

- 30-wrestler roster cap and a $15M starting budget.
- Up-to-30-pick initial draft and up-to-10-pick WrestleMania re-draft.
- Seeded roster with no CAWs, plus added wrestlers and Divas, and Legends expanded from SvR 2007/2008.
- Weight-class rebalance.
- Open manager booking.
- Condition-based four-tier morale.
- Defection rules and minor/serious injuries.
- Automatic title vacancies and full title histories.
- Survivor Series and WrestleMania signature matches.
- 10-bar fatigue with injury zones and named injuries.
- Match outcome screen with finish events (Section 20).
- Match specialties from SvR 2007/2008 (Section 21).
- Special stipulations (Section 22).
- Promo success rates, 5-for-5 trades, and an interbrand WrestleMania card (Section 23).
- Card builder Randomize and Preset tools (Section 24).

Each is marked _(house rule)_ where it appears.

**Deferred for later design:** Popularity system, Match Rating system, and the Power 25 ranking (Section 14). These are referenced structurally below (they're inputs to fan-support/financial outcomes) but their actual formulas/mechanics are intentionally left unspecified until we design our own approach.

## 1. Setup

- Player picks a brand: **Raw** or **SmackDown!** (Raw controls the Women's Championship + gets Elimination Chamber at New Year's Revolution; SmackDown controls the Cruiserweight title + gets Buried Alive at No Mercy _(house rule)_).
- _(House rule, web build)_ A new game is an **unsaved career** until the player picks **Save career** from the top bar. Only saved careers appear under Saved careers, and progress is kept as it happens once saved. The main menu offers to resume or discard the latest unsaved career, and starting another new game discards it.
- Starting budget: **$15,000,000** *(house rule; original was $10M)*. Granted once, at the initial draft of a new game. It is **not** re-granted at a WrestleMania reset; the running balance carries over.
- Roster cap: **30 wrestlers** _(house rule; original was 20)_. Superstars, Divas, and Legends all count against this.
- **Initial draft** _(house rule)_: each GM may make **up to 30 picks** (i.e. fill to the cap). A GM may stop early and fill remaining spots later via Free Agency.
    - Draft order (who picks first) is randomized each new game; picks alternate between brands.
    - Every wrestler not drafted goes into the Free Agency pool.
    - _(House rule)_ **Legends can't be drafted** in the initial draft. They stay in the Free Agency pool and can be signed once the draft is over (`gm.draft.legends_draftable`).
    - _(House rule)_ **Rival GM draft focus:** on each pick the rival GM rolls 1–100 (`gm.draft.ai_roll_max`). On **60–100** (`gm.draft.ai_focus_from`) it picks at random from its brand's colour-coded division: **Raw → Divas**, **SmackDown → Cruiserweights and Light Heavyweights**. On a lower roll, or when none of those are left that it can afford, it picks at random from the whole affordable pool.
- After drafting, the GM assigns each brand's championships to wrestlers on the roster. Each assignment opens **reign #1** of that title's history (see Section 16).
    - _(House rule)_ Titles are decided **one at a time, in tier order**: (1) the world title (WWE / World Heavyweight), (2) the second-tier title (Intercontinental / United States), (3) the tag titles (World Tag Team / WWE Tag Team), (4) the special title (Women's / Cruiserweight). For each one the GM either crowns a champion or **leaves it vacant**.
    - _(House rule)_ **A wrestler can hold only one title** when titles are awarded (after the draft, or when awarding a vacant title later). Titles won in matches aren't limited.
    - The rival GM always crowns a champion for each of its titles, picking at random among eligible wrestlers. It fills the special title first so its few eligible wrestlers aren't used up, and only leaves a title vacant if nobody eligible is left.
- One show is booked per week; PPV weeks require booking **both** the weekly show and the PPV card.

## 2. Contracts

- Contract lengths available: **5 weeks, 3 months, 6 months, 9 months, 1 year**. In weeks, using 1 month = 4 weeks: **5, 12, 24, 36, 52**.
- Shorter contracts = cheaper up front but higher recurring weekly cost; longer contracts = expensive up front, cheaper to retain per week.

### Weekly contract tick _(house rule)_

- Every contract stores `weeks_remaining`. During week resolution, after the show and any PPV air, every signed wrestler's `weeks_remaining` **decrements by 1**.
- Injured, sitting-out, and unused wrestlers still tick down. The clock runs whether or not they're booked.
- Weekly upkeep (`weekly_cost`) is charged in the same step and appears in Financials.
- **At 0 weeks**, the contract expires:
    - The wrestler is released to Free Agency.
    - Any title is vacated with reason `contract_expired` (Section 16), and any Money in the Bank briefcase is voided.
- **Renegotiating** at any time before expiry sets a new length and resets `weeks_remaining`. It also clears a contract extension morale complaint (Section 15).
- The tick runs in a single transaction, so it can't double-decrement if week resolution is retried. Week resolution is idempotent per `week_number`.

### Expiring Contracts email _(house rule)_

- Each week, if any wrestler on the brand has **4 or fewer weeks** remaining, the GM gets one **Expiring Contracts** digest email. This is one email listing everyone, not one email per wrestler.
- **Template:**
    - **From:** JR (Raw) / Tazz (SmackDown)
    - **Subject:** Contracts expiring soon — {count} superstar(s)
    - **Body:** a table with one row per wrestler: Name · Weeks left · Weekly cost · Morale · Champion? · Actions: **Renegotiate** / **Release**.
- Rows are sorted by weeks left. Rows at **1 week** are highlighted red as "Expires after this week".
- Champions are flagged, since expiry vacates their title.
- Opening the email links straight to each wrestler's contract negotiation screen.
- A follow-up **Contract Expired** email lists anyone whose contract hit 0 that week and confirms they went to Free Agency.

### Tests

- `weeks_remaining` decrements by exactly 1 per resolved week, including for injured and unbooked wrestlers.
- Re-running resolution for the same week doesn't decrement twice.
- At 0, the wrestler moves to Free Agency, titles are vacated (`contract_expired`), and briefcases are voided.
- Renegotiation resets `weeks_remaining` and clears the contract extension complaint.
- The digest email is generated only when someone has ≤4 weeks left. It lists all of them, sorted, with champions flagged.
- The Contract Expired email lists exactly the wrestlers who expired that week.
- Superstars can be released, traded, or picked up via Free Agency at any time; roster changes from Free Agency don't take effect until the following week.

## 4. Card Building

- **6 match slots** per regular show, **2 promo slots** — PPVs drop to **5 match slots** (no promos on PPV cards).
- Each slot: choose match type first, then assign 2–6 wrestlers to it, then optionally add one special stipulation (Section 22).
- Each wrestler's fatigue bars show next to their name while booking, with the projected fatigue after the match and the injury zone it lands in.
- Match type selection screen shows live **Cost/Profit** (most matches are free; specialty/gimmick matches can cost $1,000–$55,000; see the Section 10 price list) and a **fatigue** indicator for the match type.
- **No limit** on how many matches a wrestler can be booked in on one card _(house rule)_. Wrestlers booked in more than one match are flagged (shown in yellow), since each match adds fatigue and injury risk.
- **Seriously injured wrestlers can't be booked at all**, as competitor or manager. Minor-injured wrestlers can be booked normally. Wrestlers sitting out a blocked defection (Section 8) also can't be booked.
- Titles can be put on the line only if the current champion(s) are already in the match; done by highlighting the match on the card screen and cycling left/right to pick which title is defended.
- **Each title can be defended only once per card.** The title cycler skips titles already picked in another match (saved or not), and saving a second match with the same title is rejected.
- Stipulations (Section 22) are picked the same way: cycling left/right through the ones the match can take.
- **Card screen** _(2026-10-20)_: the match type is picked in two steps (category, then variant), and the full name shows under it (e.g. "6-Man Tag → Table Tornado 6-Man Tag Elimination"). Wrestler, manager and special referee slots read **VACANT** until filled. A slot with unsaved edits is tinted orange; **Discard** puts it back to its last saved booking. **Clear** (matches and promos) asks for confirmation. A progress bar tracks how many matches are booked.
- **Randomize** (one slot) and **Preset** (whole card) can fill matches automatically (Section 24).

### Managers _(house rule)_

- **Anyone on the brand's roster can be booked as a manager** in any "w/Manager" match variant, as long as they aren't competing in that same match.
- Jimmy Hart is treated like every other wrestler: no default manager role _(house rule)_.
- A wrestler can manage one match and compete in other matches on the same card. For example, they could manage Match 1, then compete in Match 2 and the main event.
- Assumption to confirm: managing adds no fatigue but counts as being used for morale (Section 15).

## 5. Promo System (2 slots/week, not available on PPVs)

| Promo | Cost/Profit | Success rate | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New Superstar | −$50,000 | 75% | Only usable on a freshly-signed/traded-in wrestler; builds popularity without a match. |
| Continue Rivalry | −$2,000 | 90% | Keeps an existing rivalry's heat alive without a match that week. |
| Start a Rivalry | $0 | 85% | Pairs two wrestlers into a rivalry without booking them together. |
| Inactive Superstar | −$2,000 | 90% | Slows popularity decay for a wrestler who isn't being used. |
| Injury Return | −$50,000 | 80% | Restores popularity lost while a wrestler was out injured. |
| Advertising | +$150,000 | 100% | Guaranteed flat payout next week. |
| Merchandise Sponsorship | + variable | 100% | Always pays; the amount is low at first and scales up the more consistently it's used. |
| Main Event Hype | −$50,000 | 75% | Boosts the rating of that week's main event. |
| PPV Hype | −$10,000 | 80% | Raises the upcoming PPV's overall rating; stacks with repeated use. |
| Interference Promotion | −$50,000 | **50%** | Sabotages the rival show, lowering their rating that week. |
| Slander Promotion | −$50,000 | **50%** | Attacks the rival show's reputation — **backfires automatically if used more than once in 4 weeks.** |
| Title Match Hype _(added 2026-10-20)_ | −$10,000 _(assumption)_ | 80% _(assumption)_ | Hypes a title match booked on this card. |
| Comeback Reminder _(added 2026-10-20)_ | −$2,000 _(assumption)_ | 90% _(assumption)_ | Keeps an injured star's popularity up while they're out. |
| Interview Space _(added 2026-10-20)_ | −$2,000 _(assumption)_ | 90% _(assumption)_ | A sit-down interview that builds a wrestler's popularity. |
| Diva Bikini Contest _(added 2026-10-20)_ | −$10,000 _(assumption)_ | 85% _(assumption)_ | Two Divas in a bikini contest to build popularity. |

### Success rates _(house rule)_

- Each promo rolls against its success rate using the save's seeded RNG. Rates live in config.
- Promos that target the rival show (Interference, Slander) are fixed at **50%**. The other rates are starting estimates: guaranteed-money promos are 100%, cheap upkeep promos are high, and expensive popularity or hype promos are lower.
- **On failure:** the cost is still paid and the promo has no effect. Slander overuse is the exception: it always backfires.
- Interference and Slander results always make the neutral News headline (Section 19), whether they succeed or fail.

_(These promo effects reference "popularity" and "rating" as their targets — the underlying formulas for both are deferred, per the note at the top of this doc.)_

### Who can run each promo _(house rule, 2026-10-20)_

A promo nobody on the roster can do is greyed out on the card screen, and booking rejects it.

| Promo | Available when / who it targets |
| --- | --- |
| New Superstar | A wrestler who joined the brand within `new_superstar_window_weeks` (2). |
| Continue Rivalry | Two wrestlers already in an active rivalry with each other. Greyed out with no rivalries. |
| Start a Rivalry | Two wrestlers who aren't seriously injured and aren't already in any active rivalry. |
| Inactive Superstar | A seriously injured wrestler. |
| Injury Return | A wrestler whose serious injury ended within `injury_return_window_weeks` (2, _assumption_). |
| Comeback Reminder | A seriously injured wrestler who has been out for more than 3 weeks. |
| Interview Space | Any wrestler who isn't seriously injured. |
| Diva Bikini Contest | Two Divas who aren't seriously injured. |
| Interference Promotion | One non-Diva superstar who isn't seriously injured. |
| Title Match Hype | A championship with a **saved** title match on this card. |
| Advertising, Merchandise Sponsorship, Main Event Hype, PPV Hype, Slander Promotion | Always available. |

### Placement and promo fatigue _(house rule, 2026-10-20)_

- Each promo airs **before a chosen match**: Before Match 1 through Before Match N−1, or Before Main Event (the default).
- **Interference Promotion** and **Start a Rivalry** add **2 fatigue bars** to their wrestlers (both wrestlers for Start a Rivalry), success or fail. The injury roll uses the fatigue going into the promo, exactly like a match (Section 6), so a promo at 100% means a serious injury. The extra fatigue counts going into the wrestler's later matches that night.
- _(Assumption)_ If a Title Match Hype's title match is no longer on the card when the show airs, the promo fizzles: the cost is still paid and it has no effect.

## 6. Fatigue & Injury

### Fatigue _(house rule)_

- Fatigue runs from 0% to 100% and is shown in the UI as **10 bars**, one bar per 10%.
- Every match type has a fatigue cost in bars (multiples of 10%). Double-booking a wrestler on the same card adds both costs.
- Fatigue caps at 100%. Recovery while resting (weeks not booked) is TBD and goes in config.

| Fatigue going into the match | Bars | Injury zone | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–70% | 0–7 | Safe (green) | No injury risk. |
| 80–90% | 8–9 | Minor risk (yellow) | Competing rolls for a **minor** injury. Odds TBD (config). |
| 100% | 10 | Serious risk (red) | Competing in any match that adds fatigue gives a **guaranteed serious injury**. |

- Example: a wrestler at 100% booked in a match that adds 10% gets a serious injury.
- Interpretation to confirm: fatigue is checked going into the match. A wrestler who reaches 100% during a match isn't injured by that match, but their next booking is.

### Injury severity _(house rule)_

| Severity | Duration | Can compete? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Minor** | 1–4 weeks | Yes. Can be booked as normal. |
| **Serious** | 5 weeks to 1 year (52 weeks) | No. Can't be booked to compete or manage. |

- **Escalation:** a minor injury can become serious. For now this is an RNG roll each time a minor-injured wrestler competes, using the save's seeded RNG; odds are TBD (config). An escalated injury takes the label from the "Escalates to" column and rolls a fresh duration in that label's range.
- Injured weeks don't count toward the inactivity morale complaint (Section 15).
- A champion's injury can vacate the title (Section 16).
- Injured wrestlers who aren't given an Inactive or Injury Return promo lose popularity while out (exact popularity mechanic: deferred).

## 7. Financials

- Weekly report of income vs. contract upkeep and promo spend.
- Line items also include special match costs and **Loser Leaves Show refunds** (Section 22).
- Running a deficit too long forces a choice: run Advertising/Merchandise promos, downgrade to cheaper match types, or release roster members.

## 8. Defection / Rival GM Interaction

- **Eligibility:** a wrestler at **Unsatisfied** or **Upset** morale (Section 15) can attempt to defect to the rival brand.
- **Weekly check:** each eligible wrestler rolls for a defection attempt during week resolution, with higher odds when Upset. The odds go in config and are TBD. An attempt sends an Email to their GM and creates a News item.
- **Outcome when the rival roster has room (under 30):** the wrestler transfers to the rival brand.
    - They keep their contract.
    - Any title they hold is vacated with reason `defected` (Section 16).
    - A Money in the Bank briefcase is voided.
- **Outcome when the rival roster is full (30):** the transfer is blocked. The wrestler **can't be booked for the next 2 weeks**, as a competitor or as a manager.
    - Assumption to confirm: those 2 weeks don't count toward the inactivity complaint, like injury weeks, so a blocked defection doesn't push morale lower on its own.

### Trades _(house rule)_

- Trades are proposed directly between brands via the Propose Trade menu.
- **Up to 5 wrestlers per side.** Sides don't have to be equal (e.g. 3-for-1), and there's no auto-balancing, so lopsided trades are possible in either direction.
- **Not tradeable:** current champions, including tag champions, and seriously injured wrestlers.
- **Roster cap check:** the trade is rejected if either roster would end up over 30 after it completes. The UI shows each brand's resulting roster size before the offer is sent.
- A traded Money in the Bank holder's briefcase is voided (Section 10).

## 15. Morale _(house rule)_

Every wrestler has one of four morale states: **Happy**, **Content**, **Unsatisfied**, **Upset**. Morale is driven by four complaint conditions, based on how SvR 2006 behaves.

### Conditions

| Complaint | Content | Unsatisfied | Upset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Losing streak** | 3 losses in a row | 5 in a row | 7 in a row |
| **Contract extension demand** (triggered by a winning streak) | Wants an extension at a 10+ win streak | Desperately wants one at 15+ | 18+ |
| **Title match demand** | No title match booked for over 1 month | 2+ months | 3+ months |
| **Inactivity** (not counting injury weeks) | Unused for 2 weeks in a row | 4 weeks | 8 weeks |

### Rules

- **Happy** means no active complaints.
- Each complaint tracks its own level. A wrestler's morale is the **worst** level across their active complaints.
- **1 month = 4 in-game weeks** for the title match demand, so 4 / 8 / 12 weeks. Keep all thresholds in config.
- The title match demand applies only if the brand has a title the wrestler is eligible for. For example, SmackDown Divas have no title, and the Cruiserweight title is limited to Cruiserweights and Light Heavyweights.
- Each new complaint level sends an Email to the GM, such as "{wrestler} wants a title shot".
- Unsatisfied and Upset wrestlers can attempt to defect (Section 8).

### Clearing complaints (assumptions to confirm)

- **Losing streak:** clears on a win.
- **Contract extension:** clears when the GM extends the contract. It persists even if the streak later ends.
- **Title match:** clears when the wrestler is booked in a title match, win or lose.
- **Inactivity:** clears when the wrestler is booked, as a competitor or as a manager.
- When a complaint clears, morale is recalculated from the remaining complaints.
- Newly signed and newly drafted wrestlers start Happy.
- Weeks spent seriously injured, or sitting out a blocked defection, don't count toward the inactivity or title match clocks.

Every championship on both brands keeps a full lineage. That covers all 8 titles: WWE, Intercontinental, World Tag Team, Women's, World Heavyweight, United States, WWE Tag Team, and Cruiserweight.

### Rules

- **Reign #1** of each title is created when the GM assigns it after the initial draft. Its `won_via` is `awarded`.
- **A new reign** starts whenever a title changes hands in a match where it was defended. The record links to the show and match.
- **Successful defenses** increment the current reign's `defenses` count.
- **Automatic vacancies** _(house rule)_ close the current reign with a reason:
    - `defected` — the champion defects to the rival brand.
    - `injured` — the champion is injured for more than 3 weeks. This always covers serious injuries (5+ weeks), including a minor injury that escalates. _(To confirm: read literally, a 4-week minor injury also vacates, even though the champion can still compete. The spec currently vacates only on serious injuries.)_
    - `loser_leaves` — the champion loses a Loser Leaves Show match (Section 22).
    - The title is vacated as soon as the injury is assigned, because its duration is known up front.
- **Roster departures** also vacate, because titles are brand-locked and never travel with the wrestler. Champions can't be traded or re-drafted, so neither of those ever vacates a title.
    - `released` — the champion is released.
    - `contract_expired` — the champion's contract runs out.
- **Tag titles:** if either member of a champion team triggers any of the above, the team's title is vacated.
- **Vacant periods** appear as their own rows in the lineage until the GM awards the title again (`won_via: awarded`) or it's won in a match (`won_via: vacancy_match`).
- **Tag titles** are held by a `TagTeam`. Each reign stores a snapshot of the members' names, so the history stays correct if the team later splits.
- **History never resets.** Reigns persist across WrestleMania resets, so the lineage spans every season of a save.

### Views

- **Title lineage** (one per title): reign #, holder, date won (season/week), event, how it was won (match type), date lost, weeks held, defenses, end reason.
- **Hall of Champions** (both brands): current champions of all 8 titles, side by side by brand.
- **Wrestler title history**: every reign a wrestler has held, on any title, across both brands. A wrestler traded from Raw to SmackDown keeps their Raw reigns on their page.
- **Records**: longest reign, most reigns, most combined defenses, and shortest reign. Show per title and across all titles.

### Data model

- **TitleReign**: championship_id, brand, reign_number, holder_type (wrestler / tag_team / vacant), holder_id (nullable), holder_snapshot (names at the time), season, won_week, won_show_id (nullable), won_match_id (nullable), won_via (match / awarded / vacancy_match / mitb_cash_in), lost_week (nullable while active), lost_show_id, lost_match_id, end_reason (active / lost_match / defected / injured / loser_leaves / released / contract_expired), defenses
- `Championship.current_holder` becomes a pointer to the active TitleReign rather than a wrestler id.

## 17. Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React + TypeScript.
- **Backend:** Laravel with MySQL.
- **Wiring:** Either Inertia.js (React TS pages served by Laravel routes, simpler auth and routing) or a separate SPA calling a Laravel JSON API with Sanctum. Inertia is the lower-overhead choice unless the frontend needs to be deployed separately.
- **Game logic lives in Laravel.** Week resolution, match sims, fatigue, injury rolls, morale updates, title changes, and finances run server-side as service classes. The frontend only builds cards and displays results. This keeps saves authoritative and makes 2-player mode possible later.
- **Seeded RNG per save**, stored on the save record, so any week can be replayed for debugging.
- **Shared types:** Generate TypeScript types from Laravel models and enums (weight classes, morale, injury severity, match types, end reasons) so both sides agree on the values.

## 20. Match Outcomes _(house rule)_

When a show airs, each match resolves in card order. Each result **flashes on screen** as an outcome card before moving to the next match. Confirm with A, Enter, or a click (Section 18).

### Outcome card contents

- **Winner(s)** and loser(s).
- **Method:** pinfall, submission, count-out, disqualification, or the match type's own win condition (ladder retrieval, cage escape, first blood, Last Man Standing 10-count, etc.).
- **Finish event:** a short story beat explaining the finish, when one occurred.
- **Title banner:** "NEW CHAMPION" or "AND STILL…".
- **Stipulation result:** for example, "Earns a title shot next week" or "Leaves SmackDown".
- **Placeholders:** the match rating and fan support delta (deferred).

### Finish events (initial 5)

| Event | What happens | Method | Allowed in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Referee Attacked** | The losing wrestler strikes the referee, so the opponent wins by disqualification. | DQ | Matches with disqualifications |
| **Rival Interference** | A wrestler in an active rivalry with the loser, and not booked in the match, interferes and costs them the match. | Pinfall (no-DQ matches) or DQ (others) | Any match where the loser has an active rival on either roster |
| **Foreign Object** | A wrestler uses a weapon. In DQ matches the user is disqualified; in no-DQ matches it sets up a legal pinfall win. | DQ or pinfall | Any |
| **Count-Out** | The fight spills outside and one wrestler fails to beat the referee's count. | Count-out | Matches with count-outs |
| **Cheap Roll-Up** | A wrestler steals the win with a roll-up, holding the tights or with feet on the ropes, and the referee misses it. | Pinfall | Matches decided by pinfall |

Candidates for later: manager distraction (fits the open manager booking in Section 4), referee bump, and referee stoppage.

### Rules

- **Titles change hands only on a decisive win.** A DQ or count-out loss means the champion retains, the same as real WWE rules. This creates the `retained_by_dq` News event.
- Each match type carries `dq_enabled` and `countout_enabled` flags, so finish events only appear where they make sense. For example, there's no Referee Attacked in a Hardcore or TLC match.
- **For now, outcomes are randomized** with the save's seeded RNG:
    1. Each side gets an equal base weight.
    2. Match specialties add a bonus weight (Section 21).
    3. The winner is rolled.
    4. Then a finish event is rolled, with a "clean finish" chance in config.
- This is a placeholder. The real outcome model will be designed alongside Popularity and Match Rating.

### Data model

- **MatchResult**: match_slot_id, winner_side, winners[], losers[], method (enum: pinfall / submission / countout / dq / match_specific), finish_event (nullable enum: referee_attacked / rival_interference / foreign_object / countout / cheap_rollup), interferer_id (nullable), title_changed (bool), stipulation_outcome (nullable), rng_seed_step

## 22. Special Stipulations _(house rule)_

A stipulation is an optional clause added to a match on the card builder, which changes what the result means. **Max one stipulation per match.** For now, every match type can take a stipulation; restrictions may come later.

**DQ and count-out rule (all stipulations):** a contender who wins by DQ or count-out is treated as **losing to the champion(s)** for stipulation purposes.

### Champion(s) vs. Contender(s)

- **Format:** always 1 vs 1 (singles title) or 2 vs 2 (tag title). The champion(s) face a contender, or a contending team, **with the title not on the line.**
- **Contender wins** (by pinfall, submission, or the match's own win condition): they earn a title match against the champion(s) on the **next weekly show or PPV, whichever comes first.** This creates a `TitleShot` obligation.
- **Injury delay:** if a contender is **seriously** injured when the shot comes due, it moves to the first show or PPV after they recover. Minor injuries don't delay it.
- If the title becomes vacant before the shot is booked, the shot is voided. _(Assumption to confirm.)_
- The card builder shows the obligation as a required match, and the card can't air until the title match is booked.
- **Champion wins:** nothing changes.
- A DQ or count-out win by the contender counts as a **champion win**, so no title shot is earned.

### Loser Leaves Show

- The losing wrestler is **released to Free Agency** right after the show.
- In a tag match, the whole losing team leaves. Only 1v1 and 2v2 matches can take this stipulation, so the "loser" is never ambiguous. _(Assumption to confirm.)_
- **Contract refund:** the loser's remaining contract (weeks left × weekly cost) is credited back to their brand's budget.
    - It appears in **Financials** as a "Loser Leaves Show refund" line item.
    - It creates a **News** headline (`loser_leaves`).
- Any title the loser holds is vacated (`loser_leaves`), and any Money in the Bank briefcase is voided.
- **Re-sign lockout:** neither brand can sign them for **1 month (4 weeks)** after they leave.

### Last Chance Match

- **Availability:** this stipulation can only be booked when **all** of these are true:
    1. The champion(s) and contender(s) have met in **at least 2 title matches during the current reign.**
    2. Those matches had **exactly the same participants**: the same 1v1, or the same 2v2 teams.
    3. **The champion(s) won every one of them.** A DQ or count-out win by the contender counts as a champion win.
- **Qualifying meetings:** any match with extra participants doesn't count toward the 2.
    - Example: Batista (World Heavyweight Champion) beats The Undertaker in a TLC match, then in a Hardcore match. On the third meeting, the GM can book Last Chance, for example in a Submission match.
    - If the second meeting had been a Triple Threat with Edge added, it wouldn't count. Last Chance wouldn't be available yet.
- Match types can differ between meetings.
- **Effect:**
    - If the contender wins, they become champion as normal.
    - If they lose (their 3rd or later loss to this champion), they **can't be booked in any title match against the same champion(s) until the title changes hands.**
    - Example: The Undertaker loses a Last Chance match to Batista. As long as Batista is World Heavyweight Champion, Undertaker can't be booked in a title match against him. If Edge then beats Batista for the title, the lockout ends and Undertaker can challenge Edge.

### Data model

- **MatchSlot.stipulation:** nullable enum (champion_vs_contender / loser_leaves_show / last_chance).
- **TitleShot:** championship_id, contender_ids[], champion_ids[], earned_match_id, due_by (next show or PPV), fulfilled_match_id (nullable), status (pending / fulfilled / voided).
- **Last Chance eligibility** is computed from TitleReign defenses plus MatchResult participants, so it needs no extra table.
- **Last Chance lockout:** championship_id, wrestler_ids[], reign_id. This record blocks booking those wrestlers into title matches for that reign, and it lapses automatically when the reign ends.
- **Re-sign lockout:** stored on the Free Agency entry as `unsignable_until_week` (Loser Leaves Show).

## 23. WrestleMania Card _(house rule)_

WrestleMania is a single **10-match** interbrand card instead of two separate brand cards.

| Slots | Who books it | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | **Raw** | Any matches, booked by the Raw GM. |
| 3 | **SmackDown** | Any matches, booked by the SmackDown GM. |
| 1 — **Main Event** | The brand of the **Royal Rumble winner** | Always that brand's **world title match**: Royal Rumble winner vs. their brand's world champion (WWE Championship on Raw, World Heavyweight Championship on SmackDown). |
| 3 | **Interbrand series** | Raw vs. SmackDown, non-title. Participants are random. |

### Main event

- **Injury fallback:** if the world champion or the Royal Rumble winner is **seriously** injured, the main event becomes a normal match slot. The Royal Rumble winner's brand books it like any other match.
- **Where the Royal Rumble winner comes from:** the 30-man interbrand Royal Rumble on the Royal Rumble PPV, with 15 entrants per brand (Section 10).

### Interbrand series

The three matches are fixed:

1. **1 vs 1**
2. **2 vs 2**
3. **Money in the Bank (6-man ladder):** 3 from Raw and 3 from SmackDown. The winner also gets the briefcase for their own brand's world title (Section 10).

- **Participants are drawn at random** from each brand's bookable non-champions, so seriously injured and sitting-out wrestlers are excluded. The 1v1 and 2v2 use the Normal variants. Titles are never on the line. _(Random participants are an assumption to confirm.)_
- The brand that wins **2 of the 3** matches wins the series. Money in the Bank counts for the brand of the wrestler who retrieves the briefcase.
- **Prize — two special tags for the post-WrestleMania re-draft (Section 13):**
    - **Exemption tag:** protect one of the winning brand's own non-champions from being drafted.
    - **Hand-pick tag:** manually choose one non-champion from the rival roster. It replaces one random pick, so the winner gets 9 random picks plus this 1 manual pick.

### Data model

- **InterbrandSeries**: season, match_ids[3], raw_wins, smackdown_wins, winner_brand, exemption_wrestler_id (nullable), handpick_wrestler_id (nullable)
- **Season.royal_rumble_winner_id** sets which brand owns the main event slot.

## 24. Card Builder Tools: Randomize & Preset _(house rule)_

Two shortcuts on the card builder. Both **propose** a booking in a preview modal. Nothing is saved until the GM confirms, and the GM can re-roll or cancel.

### Randomize (one match slot)

A **Randomize** button sits on each match slot.

- **Match type:** if the slot already has one chosen, it's kept and only the participants are randomized. If the slot is empty, a match type is also rolled. _(Assumption to confirm.)_
- **Participants** are drawn from the **bookable pool**, the brand's roster minus:
    1. Seriously injured wrestlers. Minor-injured wrestlers stay in the pool.
    2. Wrestlers already booked anywhere on this card, as competitor or manager. Manual booking still has no per-card limit (Section 4); this restriction is only for the randomizer.
    3. Wrestlers sitting out a blocked defection (Section 8).
- **Match type rules still apply:**
    - Competitor count for the type (2–6).
    - Divas-only types use only Divas.
    - PPV-exclusive types appear only on their PPV.
    - Tag types use tag teams or pairs.
- **Minimum pool:** Randomize needs **at least 2 bookable wrestlers**, plus enough to fill the rolled or chosen match type.
    - If the pool is too small, no booking is proposed. The modal shows an error, such as "Not enough available wrestlers to book this match (need 2, have 1)."
    - If the pool can't fill a chosen 6-man type, the error names the shortfall.
- **Title matches:** Randomize may make the match a title match only if **that title isn't already on this week's card**. The current champion(s) must be in the match, and every challenger must be eligible:
    - Divas only for the Women's title.
    - Cruiserweight or Light Heavyweight males only for the Cruiserweight title.
    - No one under a Last Chance lockout against that champion (Section 22).
    - Once Power 25 ships, its contender rule also applies.
- Randomize only fills match slots. It's meant to pre-fill slots when booking is hard. It never adds a stipulation (those are added manually) and never assigns managers.

### Preset (whole card)

A **Preset** button on the card header fills the card using the same rules as Randomize.

- **Respects existing bookings:**
    - Slots the GM already filled are kept.
    - Fixed slots are left alone: the WrestleMania main event and interbrand series, the Royal Rumble match, and the Survivor Series main event.
    - Preset fills only the **empty** match slots. Promo slots are left alone.
- **Obligations first:** a pending `TitleShot` (Section 22) is booked before anything else.
- **Fills slots in card order.** Each filled slot removes its wrestlers from the pool for the next slot. Each title can appear at most once on the card.
- **Running out of wrestlers:**
    - If the pool runs dry partway through, Preset fills what it can.
    - The modal lists the slots it couldn't fill.
    - If **no** slot can be filled, it shows the same error as Randomize.
- **Budget:** the preview shows the total cost of the proposed match types, and a warning appears if it would put the brand into deficit.

### Implementation notes

- Both run **server-side** in a Laravel service, e.g. `CardRandomizer`, with an injectable RNG. Production uses the save's seeded RNG, and tests use a fixed seed.
- **Endpoints:**
    - `POST /shows/{show}/slots/{slot}/randomize` returns a proposed slot.
    - `POST /shows/{show}/preset` returns a proposed card plus `unfilled_slots[]`.
    - Confirming calls the normal save endpoint, so validation runs exactly as for manual booking.
- Errors come back as `422` with a message the React modal displays as is.
