# Factions, Roster Cap Guard, Week Chip & Unsaved Changes

Executed **2026-10-27**.

Mostly factions (tag teams and stables). The same branch also guards the roster cap at its lowest level, makes the header's week chip a link to the week's card, warns before leaving a card with unsaved matches or promos, and lines up the header's Budget label. Those are covered after Tests, under Also in this branch.

## Why

Tag partners were always listed as "Matt Hardy & Jeff Hardy". This house rule (not in the original SvR 2006 GM Mode) adds **factions**: named tag teams and stables of 2–5 wrestlers. They're billed by the faction's name wherever they team up. Real-life factions are seeded, and new ones form in a career when 2 or 3 male superstars keep winning together. Factions are names and stats only for now. Gameplay benefits are deferred (CLAUDE.md TODOS).

## Naming (`FactionNamer`)

- **When it applies:** any 2+ members of a faction that was active in the match's week, on one side, are named as the faction. Leftover members keep their names: "Evolution & Kane".
- **Overlaps:** the faction covering the most of the side wins, then the smallest, then a tag team over a stable, then the name. So Matt & Jeff Hardy are Team Xtreme and Danny & Doug Basham are The Basham Brothers, not The Cabinet.
- **One side at a time:** given the match's sides, a faction only forms from members of one side, so the Hardys on opposite sides of a Triple Threat stay apart.
- **Where it's used:** every place that used to join a side with " & ".
    - Match summaries and finish text (`MatchResolver`).
    - Match news (`ShowResolver`).
    - Title and stipulation news (`MatchOutcomeHandler`).
    - Promo stories (`PromoStoryWriter`).
    - Quiet-week recaps.
    - `TitleReign::holderName()`, which uses the faction active when the reign was won.
    - The new tag-team title holder's name.
    - Earned title shots.
    - Lineage defense opponents.
    - The results presenters.
- **Not in booking:** the card screen and its pickers stay individual.
- **Results screens:** the presenters send `factions` (each faction's name and its members on a side) and `winners_label`. `SideNames` and Book to Win's `SideLabel` use `groupSide()` (`resources/js/lib/factions.ts`) to show the faction name (dotted underline, members on hover), with (C) / (Cpt) when any member has it. Reports use the server's labels.
- **Caching:** `FactionNamer` is a singleton that caches each save's factions. `FactionService` calls `forget()` after forming, adding to or disbanding a faction.

## Forming (`FactionFormation` week step)

- **When it runs:** right after `AirShows`.
- **Who qualifies:** for each winning side this week with 2 or 3 male competitors (no Divas), it counts the times exactly that set won as the whole winning side (`winsTogether`). At 3 or more (`gm.factions.formation_wins`), a team that isn't already one faction becomes one:
    - **Rival GM:** forms on the spot with a name drawn from `gm.factions.ai_names` (ASSUMPTION), skipping names already taken. Two members make a tag team, three a stable.
    - **Player:** gets a `faction_prompts` row. The game shell shares the first pending one, and `GmLayout` opens `FactionPromptModal` (not while a week is airing):
        - Step 1 takes the name and the type (a ‹ › cycler, defaulting by size).
        - Step 2 confirms (`ConfirmDialog`).
        - **Not now** defers until their next win together (`deferred_at_wins`).
- **Dates and record:** a custom faction's `formed_week` and `date_formed` are the week and show night of the deciding win. Its record counts from its first win together, so a new faction starts 3–0.
- **Repeat runs:** the step is idempotent for a week.

## Disbanding and the Factions page

- **Disbanding:** `POST factions/{saveFaction}/disband` (with a danger `ConfirmDialog`) sets `disbanded_week` to the current week. The faction keeps its name for earlier weeks. It's refused while a week is airing, or if the faction already disbanded.
- **The page** (`/gm/saves/{#}/factions`, nav **Factions**): two filters, **Show** (Created, the default: factions formed in this career by either brand; or All, adding the seeded ones) and **Status** (All, the default; Active; Disbanded), with tag teams and stables in separate panels. Each row shows:
    - members (name colours, brand or FA)
    - formed and disbanded dates: real-life ones for seeded factions; the in-game date and week for custom ones and for in-game disbanding
    - W–L (matches where 2+ members teamed)
    - titles won together, with current reigns marked
    - Disband

## Also in this branch

### Roster cap guard (§1)

The cap was already checked by Free Agency, the draft, trades, defections and the re-draft, but `ContractService::signDrafted` (the low-level signing they and test setups call) didn't check it, so a script could put a brand at 32/30. It now refuses a full roster with the same message as Free Agency ("The Raw roster is full (30)."), so nothing can push a brand over.

- `tests/Feature/Season/RosterCapTest.php` covers it:
    - Use WWE roster starts both brands at exactly 30.
    - A drafted signing and a Free Agency signing onto a full roster are both refused.
    - A signing that joins next week counts against the cap.
    - `GameBuilder::inSeason` refuses 31 names.
- `FactionsTest` checks after every test that no brand is over the cap. Its interbrand test signs the Hardys properly instead of setting their brand by hand.

### Week chip links to the week's card

The header's Season / Week / date chip is a link (`GamePresenter::currentCardUrl`, shared as `game.current_card_url`):

- **While the week airs:** the live results.
- **Otherwise:** the first unconfirmed card in booking order (the brand's PPV, the interbrand card, then the weekly show), or the last card once all are confirmed.
- **Outside the season:** no link.

It lightens on hover and has a tooltip. Tested in `tests/Feature/Http/WeekChipTest.php`.

### Unsaved changes on the card

Leaving a card with unsaved matches or promos asks first, naming them: "Match 2 and Promo 1 have unsaved changes. Leave this page and discard them?" (**Stay** / **Discard and leave**, a danger `ConfirmDialog`). That covers any Inertia visit to another page: the nav, the week chip, Air week.

- **How it works:** each match slot (`SlotEditor`) and promo slot (`PromoEditor`) reports itself with `useReportUnsaved` to the page's `UnsavedChangesProvider` (`components/gm/unsaved-changes.tsx`). The provider uses `useUnsavedChangesGuard` (`hooks/use-unsaved-changes-guard.ts`), which cancels Inertia's `before` event and replays the visit on confirm.
- **Not asked:** visits to the card's own URLs (saving a slot, Continue to …) and prefetches pass through.
- **Refresh or closing the tab:** the browser's own "Leave site?" prompt, through `beforeunload`. Browsers don't allow custom text there.
- **Tests:** `unsaved-changes.test.tsx` covers the wording (matches first, then promos) and that only slots still unsaved are listed. `card-builder.test.tsx` covers the dialog, Stay, Discard and leave, saving not being blocked, and the refresh prompt.

### Match sides line up on every row

Each row of sides on a match card is its own grid. With `auto` "vs" columns, a short last row (a 5-man match's second row) had one fewer "vs", so that column collapsed and its dropdowns grew wider than the row above. The "vs" columns are now a fixed `VERSUS_WIDTH` (2.5rem), so every row splits the same way. In a 5-man Battle Royal all five dropdowns measured 387px. The grid tests expect the fixed width; jsdom can't measure layout, which is how the old version got through.

### Responsive header and menu bar

The header and menu bar now size to their own width (`layouts/gm-layout.tsx`):

- **Header:** a Tailwind `@container` with three layouts.
    - One row from 72rem (`@6xl`). It needs about 1,020px, more with long PPV names.
    - From 42rem (`@2xl`), two rows: brand, week chip and Save on top, then Next PPV, Roster and Budget left-aligned underneath.
    - Below 42rem, the week chip gets its own row, at a smaller size under 28rem (`@md`).
    - "GM Mode" shows from `@3xl`.
- **Menu bar:** the 12 links need about 1,230px. Container queries can't detect overflow, so `useFits` measures a hidden, clipped copy of the links against the bar's width with a `ResizeObserver`. When they don't fit, the bar becomes a **Menu** button showing the current page and the unread email count. It opens the full list in a `PopoverPanel` with the current page marked.
- **Checked in the browser:**
    - At 1035px: two-row header, folded menu.
    - At 1200px: one-row header, folded menu.
    - At 1300px: everything on one line.
    - At 700px and 375px: the narrow layouts.
    - No sideways scroll at any of them.
- **Factions page:** its filter buttons now wrap, so Disbanded no longer runs off a phone screen.
- **Tests:** `layouts/gm-layout.test.tsx`, with faked widths and a fake `ResizeObserver`, covers all links when they fit, the Menu button (current page, unread count, the list, current page marked) when they don't, switching back when the bar grows, and the header being a size container.

### One scorer per finish, results card and ticket news

- **One scorer (house rule):** the finish text named the whole winning side ("La Résistance & Tajiri pin The Hurricane"). `MatchResolver::decide` now draws one scorer from the winners with equal odds, after the victim draw so earlier rolls stay put, and writes the finish in the singular around them ("Tajiri pins The Hurricane").
