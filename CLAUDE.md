## Game rules

A web take on SmackDown vs Raw 2006 GM Mode with house rules. The original 2006 game had no momentum bar, weekly grading or angle system.

### Setup and season
- The player runs **Raw** or **SmackDown** against an AI rival GM. Budget **$15M**, granted once at the first draft; it carries over between seasons. Roster cap **30** (Superstars, Divas and Legends all count).
- **Rosters (new game):** **Manual draft pick** (default) runs the initial draft below. **Use WWE roster** skips it: each brand signs the 30 wrestlers the catalog places on it (the real WWE rosters of Nov 14, 2005, trimmed to 30), on the same contract and fee as a draft pick, and the game goes straight to title assignment. Anyone without a brand, Legends included, starts in Free Agency.
- **Initial draft:** up to 30 picks each, random first pick, alternating. Legends can't be drafted (they wait in Free Agency). The rival GM rolls 1–100 each pick: 60+ picks from its division (Raw: Divas; SmackDown: Cruiserweights and Light Heavyweights), else anyone affordable.
- After the draft, titles are awarded in tier order (world, second tier, tag, special); the player can leave one vacant. At award time a wrestler can hold only one title. The rival always crowns someone.
- A season is 52 weeks on one continuous calendar, **April 2005 to March 2006** (week 1 = Sun Apr 3, 2005; WrestleMania = Sun Mar 26, 2006). **Weeks start on Sunday**: PPVs air Sunday, then Raw Monday and SmackDown Friday. 15 PPVs: 6 Raw, 5 SmackDown, 4 shared (Royal Rumble, WrestleMania, SummerSlam, Survivor Series). Every season reuses the same calendar.
- **Booking order:** the player books a week's cards in date order: their brand's PPV card, then the interbrand card (Rumble/Survivor Series entrants or WrestleMania), then the weekly show. "Continue to …" (with a confirmation) locks a card and opens the next; a locked card can be unlocked until the week airs. The weekly show's **Air week N** button (with a confirmation) starts the week's **live results** (see Outcomes); while the week airs it reads **Resume results**. The header's season/week chip links to the week's first unconfirmed card in that order (the last one once all are confirmed), or to the live results while the week airs (`GamePresenter::currentCardUrl`).
- **Schedule** is a Sunday-first month calendar with both brands' shows. Clicking an aired show opens its results (the rival's too); this week's cards open for booking; one of the player's own later show nights (their weekly show, their brand's PPVs, shared PPVs; never the rival's) offers **Simulate to here** (with a confirmation). Simulating books the player's cards for every week before the target with Preset rules, random promos and random Rumble entrants, and airs them normally: results, fatigue, injuries, title changes, contracts, morale, news and email all happen. It stops at WrestleMania and leaves the target week to book.
- **WrestleMania re-draft:** up to 10 random picks each from the rival's non-champions; the interbrand series winner swaps one for a manual pick and protects one of its own. The 30 cap still applies.

### Contracts and money
- Lengths 5 / 12 / 24 / 36 / 52 weeks; shorter costs less up front and more per week. Every contract ticks down 1 per week (injured or unused too) and charges weekly upkeep. At 0 the wrestler goes to Free Agency, titles vacate, briefcases void. Renegotiating resets the clock.
- An Expiring Contracts digest email lists everyone with 4 or fewer weeks left; a Contract Expired email follows.
- Free Agency signings and roster moves take effect the following week. Trades are up to 5-for-5, uneven allowed; champions and seriously injured wrestlers can't be traded; neither roster may end over 30. The rival GM answers every offer by email.
- A long deficit forces a choice: money promos, free match types only, or releases.

### Card building
- Weekly shows: **6 match slots and 2 promo slots**. PPVs: 5 match slots, no promos. Each slot: match type, then 2–6 competitors, then an optional stipulation.
- No per-card limit on bookings; double-booked wrestlers are flagged and take both fatigue costs. Seriously injured wrestlers and wrestlers sitting out (blocked defection, Buried Alive) can't be booked as anything.
- **Managers:** anyone on the roster can manage a w/Manager match they aren't competing in. Managing adds no fatigue but counts as being used. Jimmy Hart is a regular wrestler.
- **Special Referee** matches need a referee from the roster who isn't competing or managing and isn't seriously injured. The referee takes no fatigue, injury or record.
- **Titles:** a title can be on the line only if its champion(s) are in the match, and **each title only once per card**. Divas can only hold or challenge for the Women's title; the Cruiserweight title is for Cruiserweight and Light Heavyweight men.
- Wrestler pickers mark anyone booked in another match on the same card as **Booked** (still pickable). Match sides line up on a grid: up to 3 on one row, 4 as 2 × 2, more in rows of 3; on small screens everything stacks one per line with "vs" between.
- **Unsaved changes:** leaving a card with unsaved matches or promos (a nav link, the week chip, Air week) first asks, naming them, e.g. "Match 2 and Promo 1 have unsaved changes. Leave this page and discard them?" (**Stay** / **Discard and leave**). Saving a slot or reloading the card itself doesn't ask. A refresh or closing the tab gets the browser's own "Leave site?" prompt; browsers don't allow custom text there. Each slot reports itself with `useReportUnsaved` to the page's `UnsavedChangesProvider` (`components/gm/unsaved-changes.tsx`), which uses `useUnsavedChangesGuard`.
- **Randomize** (one slot) and **Preset** (empty slots) only propose bookings; confirming goes through normal validation. They draw from bookable wrestlers not already on the card, never add stipulations or managers, and book a pending title shot first.

### Match types
- Families and variants are seeded from `database/seeders/data/match_types.php`. Normal Matches merges singles, Triple Threat and Fatal 4-Way.
- **Rules per type** (`config('gm.match_rules')`): no rope breaks or count-outs in Hardcore, TLC, Table, Ladder, Battle Royal, Royal Rumble and Hell in a Cell; no ring outs in Steel Cage, Battle Royal, Royal Rumble and Elimination Chamber; no DQ or count-out in Normal Triple Threat / Fatal 4-Way; no titles in 3 vs 3 matches, Captain Fall, Handicap and Gauntlet; no stipulations in a Gauntlet (`no_stipulation_families`). **Captain Fall** (a non-gimmick match) is won by eliminating the other team's captain (first wrestler on the side) by pinfall, submission, DQ or count-out; partners can be eliminated along the way, but a captain's DQ or count-out loses it for their whole team. The results screen tags each captain **(Cpt)** in gold, like **(C)**.
- **Gauntlet (house rule, 2026-10-25; replaced Slobber Knocker):** 6 entrants in one slot, fought as 5 normal singles bouts (pinfall, submission, DQ, count-out). Entrants 1 and 2 open; each bout's winner faces the next entrant, and the 5th bout's winner wins. The record counts once (1 win for the winner, 1 loss for each of the other five), every entrant takes a flat **3 bars**, no titles, no stipulations. Bouts are stored in `gauntlet_bouts` (`MatchResolver::resolveGauntlet`); Book to Win books them one by one, in order.
- **Fatigue cost** per type is 1–5 bars; **prices** run $0–$55,000 (family defaults in `config('gm.match_type_defaults')`, per-variant overrides in the data file).
- **PPV exclusives:** Elimination Chamber (New Year's Revolution, Raw), Buried Alive (No Mercy, SmackDown; the loser sits out 4 weeks, their contract pauses, and they can't be injured by it), 30-man Royal Rumble (15 per brand, no titles, **no Divas** (house rule): random entrants skip them, `PpvCardService::setEntrants` refuses them and the entrant picker greys them out; the winner's brand gets the WrestleMania main event), Traditional 3 vs 3 Survivor Series Elimination (always that PPV's Raw vs SmackDown main event), Money in the Bank (WrestleMania, 6-man ladder, no Divas or titles; the briefcase is a world title shot, cashable on any card within 52 weeks or until next WrestleMania, voided if the holder leaves the brand).
- **WrestleMania** is one 10-match interbrand card: 3 Raw slots, 3 SmackDown slots, the main event (Royal Rumble winner vs their brand's world champion), and a 3-match random interbrand series (1v1, 2v2, Money in the Bank) whose winner gets the re-draft perks.

### Promos
- Each promo rolls its success rate with the seeded RNG; the cost is paid either way. **Success rates (house rule, 2026-10-25):** every promo succeeds (100%) except Advertising 80%, Interview Space 90%, Interference 50% and Slander 50%, plus Merchandise Sponsorship below. Interference and Slander always make the neutral news headline (except in a PPV week). Slander backfires if used more than once in 4 weeks.
- **One of each promo per wrestler (house rule):** a wrestler can't be in two promos of the same type on one card (e.g. two Interview Spaces, or Melina in both Diva Bikini Contests). `PromoService::book` refuses it, and the picker greys them out ("In Promo N"). Different promo types are fine. Save promo stays greyed out while the promo matches what's saved, and **Discard** puts it back to the saved promo, like a match.
- **Continue Rivalry and rivalry names (house rule):** Continue Rivalry has one picker listing the brand's rivalries as "A vs. B" (`RivalryPicker`), not two wrestler pickers. It always succeeds and tires both rivals 1 bar. Rivalries are always 1v1 and named alphabetically ("Jeff Hardy vs. Rob Van Dam"): a new rivalry stores the alphabetically first wrestler as `wrestler_a` (`RivalryService::alphabetical`), and the Rivalry page and picker sort older ones too.
- **Merchandise Sponsorship (house rule):** when it airs it rolls one deal with equal odds: $100,000 (80% success), $250,000 (70%), $500,000 (50%) or $1,000,000 (25%) (`gm.promos.merchandise_tiers`). A success pays that amount; a failure pays nothing. The old "grows with consecutive use" payout is gone. The card shows its odds as 25–80%.
- **Interview Space gets crashed (house rule):** when it fails for a wrestler not booked on the card, a random healthy male superstar from the same brand (not sitting out) crashes the interview and decks them (`PromoService::crashInterview`, stored as the promo's second target). If neither of them is in a rivalry yet, that starts one between them.
- `PromoEligibility` decides who can run each promo; unavailable promos are greyed out and booking rejects them. Highlights: Inactive Superstar targets someone **not booked on the card**; Interference needs a healthy non-Diva; Start a Rivalry needs two healthy wrestlers not already in a rivalry; Diva Bikini Contest needs two healthy Divas; Title Match Hype needs a saved title match on the card; Comeback Reminder needs someone seriously injured for more than 3 weeks.
- Each promo airs **before a chosen match** (default: before the main event, or the latest spot still free). **Two promos can't air back to back (house rule):** each spot holds one promo, so a spot a saved promo holds is crossed out (red X, greyed, not-allowed cursor) in the other promo slot and booking it is refused (`PromoService::takenSpots`). Interference and Start a Rivalry add **2 fatigue bars** there, Diva Bikini Contest **2** (both Divas) and Continue Rivalry **1** (both rivals), success or fail, and roll injuries like a match (`gm.promos.fatigue_bars`, seeded onto `promo_types`).
- A Title Match Hype whose title match is gone fizzles; an Inactive Superstar whose target ends up booked fizzles. Popularity and rating effects are stubs. There are 14 promos (Injury Return was dropped as a duplicate of Comeback Reminder).
- **Promo stories (house rule):** every promo's news story is written after the show airs (`PromoStoryWriter`, texts in `PromoStories`) from what happened around it. Interview Space: the wrestler's match (success or fail × won or lost); unbooked, a generic interview plus extras for their form (3+ win or loss streak, a long wait for a title match, holding a title). With nothing about their form, it falls back to the spotlight story: "{wrestler} had a sit-down interview / an interview tonight and was being goofy / enthusiastic" (each 50%), "…ready to take over and dominate the {brand} division!". A crashed interview tells how {crasher} "crashed the party and decked {wrestler} with authority", adding that it started a rivalry when it did. Otherwise a failed unbooked interview falls flat. Main Event Hype: the main event (title change, retained, the challenger's DQ/count-out win, or a non-title match), a success being the brand's headline. Title Match Hype: that title match, and where it was on the card. Every other promo: success or fail (Inactive Superstar and Title Match Hype can fizzle; Slander can backfire). Every situation has at least 4 variants, drawn with equal odds from the seeded RNG; stories that name a wrestler always name them.

### Fatigue and injuries
- Fatigue is 0–100%, drawn as 10 bars. **Injuries (house rule, 2026-10-25):** the fatigue a superstar ends a match (or a tiring promo) on decides, counted before the 100% cap (going in + the match's cost). Ending at 0–70% is safe. Ending at 80–100% always injures: 80% minor, 20% serious (`gm.injuries.serious_share`). Going over 100% (e.g. 70% into a 4-bar match = 110%) is always serious. The uncapped total is kept on the injury (`injuries.fatigue_after`). The results fatigue card shows it as all bars full with a red "110% Injury", and in the after-recovery step a seriously injured superstar shows "Serious Injury: {name}!" instead of their fatigue. A minor injury still goes through recovery, with "Minor Injury: {name}!" beside the recovered fatigue.
- **Weekly recovery (house rule):** at the end of every week **everyone recovers 2 bars** (`gm.fatigue.rest_recovery_bars`), booked or not. A successful Inactive Superstar promo gives its target **2 more** (`gm.promos.recovery_bars`). Fatigue never drops below 0. Only wrestlers in matches or fatiguing promos gain fatigue; recovery applies to everyone. Examples: A starts at 30%, wrestles a 50% match (80%), ends the week at 60% and starts the next show there. B starts at 40%, isn't booked (still 40%), ends at 20%. An unbooked wrestler at 30% with a successful Inactive Superstar ends at 0%.
- Injuries: minor 1–4 weeks (can still compete; may escalate to serious each time they do), serious 5–52 weeks (can't be booked). Labels and durations are seeded.
- A champion's serious injury vacates the title immediately.
- The Trainer emails the GM about every injury and escalation; a seriously injured superstar emails 2 weeks before they're cleared.

### Outcomes
- Matches resolve in card order with promos in between. Outcomes are placeholders: equal base weights, plus a specialty bonus (specialists cancel out), then a finish event (Referee Attacked, Rival Interference, Foreign Object, Count-Out, Cheap Roll-Up) or a clean finish. A clean finish won by a Submission specialist is a submission.
- **Finish text (house rule, 2026-10-25):** the summary follows the type's win condition (`FinishText`) and names the one wrestler it happens to: in a match with several opponents the losing side is drawn at random, in a tag match one losing member is (Captain Fall: the captain). "Shelton Benjamin puts Rob Van Dam through a table." Battle Royals and elimination matches end with "{W} last eliminates {L} via pinfall". Buried Alive adds "He is nowhere to be seen (out for N weeks)" from `gm.buried_alive.sit_out_weeks`. A Foreign Object finish draws a `Weapon` (steel chair, sledgehammer, ring bell, steel steps); without DQs, it and a Rival Interference still end the type's way (through a table, the briefcase, the 10-count…) with that win condition as the method, not a pinfall. Titles change hands only on a decisive win (not DQ or count-out).
- **Live results (house rule):** Air week opens the results screen with the player's matches undecided. Shows air in order (PPV, interbrand, weekly shows). The rival GM's own shows always simulate on their own when their turn comes; the player's shows and the interbrand cards wait. The player decides one match at a time, in air order: the next match's **Pending Result** button opens its card, with **Book to Win** beside each side and **Simulate match**; **Simulate all** (beside Continue) decides the rest. Promos air just before the match they were placed in front of. After the last match the rest of the week runs on the same screen and Continue opens the News. Roster moves (signings, releases, trades) wait until the week is in. Simulate to here finishes a half-aired week as it stands.
- **Book to Win:** the booked side always wins. A booked **title** match always ends decisively (pinfall, submission or the type's own win condition, never DQ or count-out). Any other booked match ends by DQ or count-out **5%** of the time where the type allows them (`gm.outcomes.booked_non_decisive_chance`), else decisively. Simulated matches keep the normal odds above.
- The results screen lists every result with the promos where they aired (**Promo N**, the promo, its wrestlers, and **Succeeded** in green or **Failed** / **Fizzled** / **Backfired** in red). In a title match the champion going in is tagged **(C)**. Each result opens its full outcome card with arrows to step through. The fatigue card (going in → after the show → after recovery) appears and animates once every match is decided.

### Stipulations (max one per match, none in a Gauntlet)
- **Champion vs Contender:** 1v1 or 2v2, no title on the line; a contender win earns a title match on the next show or PPV, and the card can't air until it's booked.
- **Loser Leaves Show:** 1v1 or 2v2 only; the loser goes to Free Agency, their contract is refunded, titles vacate, and nobody can sign them for 4 weeks.
- **Last Chance:** only after the same participants have met twice in the current reign with the champion winning both; a loss locks the contender out of title matches against that champion until the title changes hands.
- A contender's DQ or count-out win counts as a champion win for every stipulation.

### Morale, defection and titles
- Morale is Happy / Content / Unsatisfied / Upset: the worst of four complaints (losing streak 3/5/7; contract demand at win streaks 10/15/18; no title match for 4/8/12 weeks; unused for 2/4/8 weeks). Injury and sit-out weeks don't count. The superstar emails the GM when a complaint reaches Unsatisfied or Upset (Content doesn't email).
- Unsatisfied and Upset wrestlers may try to defect. They transfer if the rival has room (titles vacate, briefcase voids), otherwise they sit out 2 weeks.
- Every title keeps a full lineage across seasons, including vacancies and the reason (defected, injured, Loser Leaves, released, contract expired), plus records. The lineage table shows #, Holder, Won (season and week, plus the PPV name), Reign (the holder's Nth reign with that title), How ("Awarded", or the match it was won in; vacant rows give the vacancy reason), Weeks and Defenses. Under each reign, every match it was defended in (a DQ or count-out loss included), newest first, then the match the title was won in (marked Won; an awarded title has none), each linked to that match's results.

### Rival GM
The brand nobody plays books with strategy (house rule, 2026-10-25; numbers in `config('gm.ai')`). The player's Randomize, Preset and Simulate to here keep the plain Preset rules.
- **Matches** (`AiBooker`): each rolls **85% careful** (nobody ends it above 70%) or **15% aggressive** (heavy gimmicks first, up to 110%, so injuries can happen). A slot it can't fill within the rules stays empty. Earned title shots go first.
- **Gender:** each match rolls **95/5** for intergender. At least **1 all-Diva match** per card with 2+ bookable Divas, on a random slot (match 5 or the main event when it's for the Women's title). w/Manager types get managers of the competitors' gender. Intergender matches use only 7 shapes (`IntergenderRules`): Diva managers in 1 w/Manager vs 1 w/Manager, 1 vs 1 w/Manager, 2 w/Manager vs 2 w/Manager and 2 vs 2 w/Manager (male competitors); mixed teams of 1 man + 1 Diva in 2v2 and 2v2v2; 3v3 (not Table Tornado 6-Man Tag Elimination) with the same number of Divas per team, at least 1 and not all.
- **Titles:** the main event rolls for a title match first (weekly 60%, PPV 90%), trying world, second tier, tag, special in order; other slots roll 35%. A careful slot skips a worn-out champion. A non-title 1v1/2v2 with a champion alone on a side has a 10% Champion vs Contender roll.
- **Promos** (`AiPromoPlanner`, after the matches): low on cash (at or below $500,000, or short of the renewals due in the next `save_ahead_weeks` *(assumption)*) Advertising then Merchandise; else PPV Hype 1 or 2 weeks before its own brand-only PPVs (rolled once per PPV), Interference every 4 weeks, Slander after a rolled gap (3 weeks 10%, 5 weeks 30%, 6 weeks 60%), Start a Rivalry when the rivalry plan wants one, one weighted situational promo (Title Match Hype for a title on the card, weighted world 60 / second 30 / other 10; New Superstar; Comeback Reminder; Inactive Superstar; Continue Rivalry; Main Event Hype; Interview Space; Diva Bikini Contest), and money promos fill the rest. Tiring promos stay within 70%. When the rival slanders, JR/Tazz email the player.
- **Rivalries** (`AiRivalryPlanner`): at most **7** on its brand. Below 7 it waits **2 weeks** after the last one ended, then starts one a week. At **0** it makes **at least 4** that week (up to 2 promos, the rest by booking fresh 1v1 same-gender pairs and starting them at booking). Each rivalry has a 50% chance of a 1v1 on a card. Rivalries are always same-gender.
- **Roster** (`AiRosterManager`, week step `RivalRosterUpkeep` after morale): renews contracts with 4 or fewer weeks left for 52 weeks (champions and its focus class first; short of money, a shorter deal, dipping into the $500,000 reserve if it must *(assumption)*), and signs free agents up to 28 (title fillers, then its focus class), keeping the reserve.
- **Trades** (`AiTradeJudge`): each wrestler is worth 1, +2 for a Legend, +1 for a former champion, +1 for its focus class. A fair offer that keeps every title fillable is accepted 90% of the time, anything else 5%.
- **Money in the Bank:** it cashes in when its world champion goes in at 60%+ fatigue or the briefcase expires within 4 weeks.
- **Draft:** it stops chasing its focus class after 12 picks, and its last picks fill any title nobody on its roster is eligible for.

### News and email
- News is a weekly report: one page per played week from week 1 (quiet weeks show "no headline/story this week"), with 3 headlines (Raw, WWE, Smackdown), 4 stories (2 per brand) and a ticker. **Only aired weeks (house rule):** the News page lists weeks before the current one (`NewsComposer::weeks`); news recorded during the current week (a signing, a trade) waits until that week has fully aired, live results included. Every card shows its headline plus a short summary of why it happened.
- Everything newsworthy in a week goes into one pool: every match result, title changes and defenses, Champion vs Contender shots, promo results, rivalries (started, heating up, 10 hot weeks, blow-off), signings, contracts with 2 weeks left, injuries and returns, upcoming PPVs. `NewsComposer` draws headlines and stories from it with the seeded RNG, weighted by priority. Brand cards only use their brand's events. The WWE card only uses cross-brand news (PPV main events, Royal Rumble winner, PPV buzz, interbrand series, trades, defections, legends, re-draft, season reset). **WWE headline precedence (house rule):** in a PPV week the PPV wins: its main event (`ppv_results`, who won, the title if one was on the line, the gimmick if it's one, e.g. "Backlash main event: John Cena defeats Edge in a Ladder match to win the WWE Championship!"), the interbrand card's main event ahead of a brand PPV's (`gm.news.ppv_main_event_priority`), and the Royal Rumble winner for the Rumble. Then a Slander or Interference result. Then other cross-brand headline news, drawn by priority (trades, defections, "{ppv} is 1 week away"…). Last, the quiet-week tickets-on-sale filler. PPV buzz (`ppv_upcoming`, 2 weeks out as a story, 1 week out as a headline) goes on the WWE card for every PPV, brand PPVs included. A plain match result gives way to bigger news from the same match. Match results rank by size, with headline precedence PPV → title → main event → gimmick: each step outranks everything below it, a gimmick match is anything but a plain singles, tag, 6-man tag or handicap match (Table tag variants are gimmicks), and a DQ or count-out win ranks a little lower without flipping a step (`gm.news.match_result_priority`). A title change always takes its brand's headline. A brand with no headline-sized news gets its biggest match as the headline (or the bigger news that replaced its result), not a random story. Whatever isn't picked runs in the ticker.
- **Title change and match result wording (house rule):** news cards already sit under their brand, so these don't name the show. A title change reads "NEW CHAMPION! {winner} defeats {loser} for the {title} via {how}!", and its summary ends with the champion looking forward to new challengers "that she/he will dethrone". It's "she" for the Women's title (the Divas' only title), "he" for every other title, and "they" for tag titles. A match result reads "{winner} defeats {loser} in decisive fashion. He/She/They made sure to get the win via {how}." A DQ or count-out win reads "… via disqualification. Not the cleanest win, but a win is a win." **Injured win (house rule, 2026-10-25):** a decisive win where a winner went in with a minor injury that's still minor once the show is over reads "Handicapped and determined, {winner} defeats {loser} in a hard-hitting match via {how} despite suffering a minor injury. Remember, an injury is not a hindrance to winning the match!" (`match_result.injured`; `ShowResolver` re-checks at the end of the show and falls back to the decisive text if the injury turned serious in a later match). DQ and count-out wins keep their own text. The summary names the match in a word: the family ("Ladder", "TLC"), or singles / Triple Threat / Fatal 4-Way for Normal Matches. {how} is the result's win condition in lower case: pinfall, submission, disqualification, count-out, or the type's own condition (briefcase retrieval, 10-count…). News recorded before this keeps its old wording (`NewsTemplates` `*.legacy`).
- **Quiet weeks (house rule):** the News page is never empty. Once a week's news is in (`QuietWeekNews`, the last step before the week advances), every card it left empty gets filler, written then so old weeks keep that week's facts. WWE headline: tickets on sale for whatever airs next (the PPV's name, or Monday Night Raw and its date). Brand headline: the brand's top champion (world, then second tier, tag, special) spent the week with fans in a city, or with no champions the roster hung out at WWE HQ, plus a budget line (positive above the $500,000 low-cash mark, a warning at or below it). Brand stories: a random superstar's last match, then the injury report for the last 26 weeks (praise if none, booking advice if any). Ticker: unhappy wrestlers, contracts within 4 weeks of running out, or all quiet. Filler has the lowest priority and is never promoted over real news; texts live in `FallbackStories`.
- Email carries private GM notices. JR (Raw) / Tazz (SmackDown): Welcome at each season start, Superstar specialties at season weeks 1 and 27, the Interference reminder after 13 unused weeks (once per stretch), Low on Cash at $500,000 or less (once every 4 weeks), contract digests, deficit and defection notices. Rival GM: trade answers (accept or decline, one superstar per line) and Slander reactions. Vince McMahon: reminders 2 weeks before SummerSlam, Survivor Series, Royal Rumble and WrestleMania, and the re-draft after WrestleMania. Trainer: every injury (who, what, how long). Superstars: morale complaints at Unsatisfied/Upset, contract up in 2 weeks, back from serious injury in 2 weeks.

### Factions (house rule, 2026-10-27)
- A faction is a **tag team** or a **stable** (`FactionType`) of **2–5 members** (`gm.factions`). The real-life ones are seeded from `database/seeders/data/factions.php` (catalog `factions` / `faction_members`, members all from the roster, Divas allowed) and copied into every new save (`save_factions` / `save_faction_members`, `FactionService::seedSave`), active from week 1. Their real formed/disbanded dates are for display only.
- **Naming:** any 2+ members of a faction active that week, teaming on one side, are billed as the faction everywhere a team is named: results and outcome cards, Book to Win, news, title lineage and records, reports. The rest of the side keeps their names ("Evolution & Kane"). Overlaps: the faction covering the most of the side, then the smallest, then a tag team over a stable, then the name (Matt & Jeff Hardy = Team Xtreme; Edge & Christian = The Brood). `FactionNamer` does it (a singleton; `forget()` after changes). Booking stays individual: the card screen and its pickers list wrestlers by name. News is written when recorded, so old stories keep their wording; a disbanded faction keeps its name for the weeks it was together.
- **Forming:** 2 or 3 male superstars (no Divas) who win **3** matches together as the whole winning side, and aren't already one faction, form a new one (`FactionFormation` week step, right after the shows). The rival GM's form on the spot with a name from `gm.factions.ai_names` *(assumption)*. The player's get a `faction_prompts` row: a pop-up asks for a name and a type, then a confirmation; **Not now** asks again after their next win together. A custom faction is dated from the show of the deciding win, and its record counts from its first win together.
- **Disbanding:** any active faction can be disbanded from the Factions page (`/gm/saves/{#}/factions`, with a confirmation), from the current week on. The page opens on the factions **created** in this career (both brands), with **All** beside it to add the seeded ones, and a status filter (All / Active / Disbanded). Each row has members, dates, record (matches where 2+ members teamed) and the titles they won together.
- **News:** `faction_formed` (brand headline), `faction_member_added` (story; nothing adds members yet), `faction_title_won` (replaces the title change when the new tag champions are a faction), `faction_title_lost` (story), `faction_interbrand_win` (WWE card) and `faction_disbanded` (story). Priorities in `gm.news.faction_priority`.
- Factions give no gameplay benefit yet (see TODOS).

## Stack
- Backend: Laravel + MySQL. All game logic lives here as service classes (week resolution, match sim, fatigue/injury, morale, contracts, titles, finances).
- Frontend: React + TypeScript via Inertia.js. The frontend books cards and displays results; it never decides outcomes.
- Tests: Pest (backend), Vitest + React Testing Library (frontend).

## Rules
- Use PHP backed enums for every enumerated value in the spec (weight class, morale, injury severity, match family, stipulation, end reason, email template…). Generate matching TS types; don't hand-write duplicates.
- All randomness goes through an injectable, seeded RNG stored on the save. Never call `rand()`/`Math.random()` directly. Tests use a fixed seed.
- Tunable numbers (odds, thresholds, durations, success rates) go in `config/gm.php`, not inline.
- Week resolution must be idempotent per `week_number` and run in a DB transaction.
- Seed data (roster, match types, injury types, titles, PPVs, promos, specialties) comes from the spec tables via seeders. The roster's `placeholder_brand` is the Nov 14, 2005 WWE roster (30 per brand, see `docs/20261024-2005-roster.md`): a manual draft ignores it, and "Use WWE roster" signs it.
- Every rule with a test list in the spec gets those tests before the feature is considered done.
- No login or user accounts for now: saves are global, `/` redirects to `/gm`, and there is no `users` table, Fortify or settings area. Don't reintroduce auth unless asked.
- A career is listed under Saved careers only after the player hits **Save career** (`saves.saved_at`). After that, progress is kept as it happens. Starting a new game deletes any never-saved career.
- Roster names are plain ring names with no nicknames (`Roddy Piper`, not `"Rowdy" Roddy Piper`). There are no managers-by-default; Jimmy Hart is a regular wrestler.
- On `/gm/docs`, a match's **rules** (DQ, Elimination, Ring out, Rope breaks, Special referee, Timed, Titles) and **win conditions** (Pinfall, Submission, Count-out, Captain fall, and the type's own condition) are separate: the Match rules and Win conditions cards sit side by side, and each match row shows only the badges that apply, sorted alphabetically. When you add a rule or win condition, add its description to the matching card in `resources/js/pages/gm/docs.tsx`.
- The `/gm/docs` **News & email** tab lists every news event from `App\Services\News\NewsCatalog` (group, card, tier; headline and summary templates come from `NewsTemplates`) and every GM email from `DocsController::emails()`. When you add a news event type, add it to `NewsCatalog` (a test fails otherwise). When you add an email, add its row to `DocsController::emails()`.
- Match-type house rules (rope breaks, ring outs, no-title types, six-competitor fatigue) live in `config('gm.match_rules')`; per-variant overrides go in `database/seeders/data/match_types.php`. Match prices: family defaults in `config('gm.match_type_defaults')`, per-variant `cost` overrides in the data file. Catalog data changes go in the seeders only; apply them with `php artisan migrate:fresh --seed` (it wipes saves, which is fine for now). Don't write data-only migrations; migrations are for schema changes.
- Fatigue and win/loss streaks are always drawn as bars, never as bare text or percentages: `<FatigueMeter>` (`components/gm/fatigue-meter.tsx`) and `<StreakMeter>` (`components/gm/streak-meter.tsx`), as on `/gm/saves/{#}/rosters/{#}`. This includes fatigue *costs* such as a match type's fatigue on `/gm/docs`.
- Name colour coding (SvR 2006 style): Cruiserweights and Light Heavyweights render blue, Divas red. Use `<WrestlerName>` from `resources/js/components/gm/wrestler-name.tsx` anywhere a wrestler name is listed.
- The card screen uses custom pickers, not `<select>`: `<WrestlerPicker>` (`components/gm/wrestler-picker.tsx`) for wrestler, manager and referee slots, and `usePopover`/`<PopoverPanel>` (`components/gm/popover.tsx`) for anything else that opens a list. Titles and stipulations are left/right cyclers (`components/gm/title-tools.tsx`).
- Clear and other destructive buttons use `gm-btn-danger` and confirm through `<ConfirmDialog danger>`.
- Custom styles live in `resources/css/gm.css` inside `@layer components`. Tailwind utilities (e.g. `text-ink-dim`) beat that layer regardless of specificity, so an override of a utility there needs `!important`.

## Architecture notes
- Catalog tables (`wrestlers`, `match_types`, `championships`, …) are shared; per-save state lives in `save_wrestlers` and friends.
- RNG: `$save->rng($stream, $week)` (Xoshiro256**). Stream keys must be stable across runs (e.g. `Show::streamKey()`), never DB ids.
- Week resolution: `WeekResolver` runs ordered `WeekStep`s after its `WeekGuard`s pass. Live results split it: `begin()` runs the steps before `AirShows` and airs up to the player's first match, `decide()` / `simulateAll()` air matches (`ShowResolver::airNext`), and the steps after `AirShows` run once everything has aired. Between requests the week's `WeekContext` data and its show RNG streams (`WeekContext::stream()`, saved with `Rng::state()`) live in `week_airings`. `resolveWeek()` still airs a whole week in one call (tests, Simulate to here), with the same results as an all-simulated live week.
- `GameRuleException` returns a 422 JSON body to fetch callers; for Inertia requests it flashes a toast and redirects back with a `game` error.
- JSON (fetch) callers of mutating card endpoints get `204`, not a redirect. A 302 after a `PUT` makes the browser re-send the `PUT`.
- Promo availability lives in `PromoEligibility`: the card screen greys promos out from it and `PromoService::book` validates against it, so the two can't disagree. Promos air before a chosen match (`promo_slots.before_position`, default the main event); `ShowResolver` resolves them in between matches, so promo fatigue counts going into later matches.
- Special Referee matches store the referee as a `MatchParticipant` with `role = referee`. Anything that loops over participants must only treat `competitor`s as competitors.
- Booking order lives in `BookingOrder` (`shows.confirmed_at` marks a card the player finished). Card mutations go through `assertEditable` in the controllers, not the services, so the AI and the simulator (`WeekSimulator`) can still book. `WeekController::advance` checks `assertReadyToAir`; `WeekResolver` itself doesn't, so tests can resolve weeks directly.
- The rival GM (`AiGm`) delegates to `AiRivalryPlanner`, `AiBooker` and `AiPromoPlanner` for its own cards (see Rival GM above); for the player's cards during Simulate to here it books with Preset rules. Tests that don't want the rival's extras call `GameBuilder::passiveRival()`. In the draft room the frontend steps the AI one pick at a time via `POST draft/ai-step`, so it can animate each pick.

## Git Conventions

- **Git**: feature branches follow `type/YYYYMMDD-description` (e.g. `enhancements/20260519-blog`, `chore/20260404-...`, `feature/...`). Commit subjects are lowercase and imperative (`add`, `fix`, `refactor`, `update`), and the first line must stay within 100 characters (it is the only part shown in summaries). PRs merge to `main`.
- **Pull requests**: always assign the repo owner [dnextreme88](https://github.com/dnextreme88) as both assignee and reviewer on every PR you create (e.g. `gh pr create --assignee dnextreme88 --reviewer dnextreme88`), so they don't have to do it manually afterward.
- **Formatting** is enforced by Pint using [pint.json](pint.json) (currently the bare Laravel preset). Run `vendor/bin/pint` before committing. Frontend formatting/lint runs through `npm run check` (vite-plus). The generated `resources/js/types/enums.ts` is excluded from it.
- **Plans**: implementation/upgrade plans live in [docs](docs), one Markdown file per plan named `{YYYYMMDD}-kebab-case-topic.md` where the date is when the plan was executed (see the existing and renamed files there for the format). Add a matching entry to [docs/README.md](docs/README.md) — link the dated filename and bold the executed date.
- **Executing a plan**: before starting the work in a plan, pull the latest `main`, then create a new branch off it with a meaningful name per the `type/YYYYMMDD-description` convention above. Do all plan work on that branch, never directly on `main`.
- **Tests** use Pest — all tests in this suite are written with Pest. [tests/Pest.php](tests/Pest.php) applies `RefreshDatabase` to every `Feature` test (in-memory sqlite); `Unit` tests stay DB-free. Build game state with the helpers in `tests/Support`: `GameBuilder` (`newSave`, `inSeason`, `wrestler`, `giveTitle`), `Card` (`show`, `book`, `type`, `singles`) and `FixedOutcomes` (scripted winners, matched by who is in the match). Arch tests ban `rand()`-family calls in `app/` and `Math.random` in `resources/js`. When you change a file, run the relevant tests (`vendor/bin/pest`); if no test covers the change, write one before finishing.
